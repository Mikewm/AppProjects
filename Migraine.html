Migraine Tracker

Monitor, manage, and understand your migraine patterns with comprehensive tracking and insights.

Health Tracking HealthKit Statistics SwiftUI

📊 Dashboard Overview

Quick snapshot of your current migraine status, duration, and pain level with monthly statistics to visualize trends and identify triggers.

📈 Episode History

Chronological log of all recorded episodes with timestamps, pain intensity ratings, and durations to track progress and share with healthcare providers.

💊 Medication Management

Manage treatments effortlessly by categorizing medications as abortive or preventive, keeping your regimen organized for better symptom control.

⚙️ Settings & Preferences

Customize your experience with HealthKit sync, daily reminders, and report exports for your physician. Personalization ensures your data works for you.

🎯 Episode Details

Dive deeper into each episode's timeline, pain intensity, and location with an intuitive interface for recording and reviewing experiences.

MoodPulse and MoodBMI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Welcome to MoodPulse and MoodBMI (“the Apps”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when using the Apps.

By using the Apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Apps are designed to minimize data collection.

Depending on features used, the Apps may collect:

  • Mood tracking entries
  • Wellness assessment responses
  • BMI-related inputs
  • Basic app analytics (if enabled in future versions) on-device only.

The Apps do not intentionally collect personally identifying information.

2. Health and Wellness Disclaimer

MoodPulse and MoodBMI are informational wellness tools only.

The Apps:

  • Do not provide medical diagnoses
  • Do not replace professional healthcare advice
  • Are not intended for emergency use
  • Should not be relied upon as medical treatment

If you are experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, contact a qualified healthcare professional or emergency services immediately.

3. Apple HealthKit

If enabled by the user, the Apps may access Apple HealthKit data.

HealthKit data:

  • Is accessed only with user permission
  • Is used solely for app functionality
  • Is not sold to third parties
  • Is not used for advertising purposes

Users may revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in Apple Health settings.

4. Data Storage

Data is only stored locally on the user’s device and within Apple-provided systems such as iCloud if enabled by the user.

Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect user information.

5. Third-Party Services

The Apps do not use third-party services including:

  • Apple App Store services
  • Apple HealthKit
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Analytics providers (if enabled)

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Limited information may only be shared:

  • When required by law
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect platform security and integrity

7. Children’s Privacy

The Apps are not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. User Controls

Users may:

  • Delete app data at any time
  • Remove the Apps from their device
  • Revoke HealthKit permissions
  • Disable notifications

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Continued use of the Apps after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
